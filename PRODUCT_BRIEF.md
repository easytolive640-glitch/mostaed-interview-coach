# مستعد – Week 1 Product Brief

## Promise

Practice realistic Arabic or English job interviews and receive clear,
actionable feedback before the real interview.

## Initial audience

- Fresh graduates applying for their first job.
- Customer-service and call-centre applicants.
- IT support, cloud, and junior DevOps applicants.

## Version 1 scope

- Arabic-first onboarding and an English-language option.
- HR, customer service, and IT/cloud interview categories.
- Five typed answers and local scoring in the free flow.
- A score, strengths, and actionable improvements.
- Planned Starter interviews have 10 text questions plus one recorded voice answer;
  planned Pro interviews have 15 text questions plus one recorded voice answer.
  Paid AI evaluation requires account and subscription verification.
- Proposed monthly Starter and Pro limits of 20 and 100 AI evaluations; final prices and limits require cost validation.

## Key screens

1. Onboarding and language selection.
2. Home and interview category selection.
3. Interview question and answer screen.
4. Results and improvement report.
5. History, profile, and upgrade screen.

## Success metrics

- At least 40% of new users complete their first interview.
- At least 25% return within seven days.
- Track actual AI and payment costs against net revenue before choosing final prices.
- First 10 paying users within 30 days of public launch.

## Not included in version 1

- CV builder or job board.
- Employer dashboards.
- Live video interviews.
- Unlimited AI usage.
